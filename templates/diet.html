NUTRITION SUPPORT

Food & Diet 🍎

General nutrition information that can support a healthy lifestyle for people living with RA.

Healthy Food Choices

🐟

Omega-3 Foods

Foods such as fish, flaxseeds and walnuts provide omega-3 fatty acids.

🥦

Vegetables

Include a variety of vegetables and nutrient-rich foods in your meals.

🍊

Fruits

Fruits provide vitamins, minerals and dietary fibre.

🌾

Whole Grains

Whole grains can be part of a balanced and varied diet.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink enough fluids throughout the day according to your individual needs.

🥗

Balanced Meals

Aim for balanced meals containing vegetables, protein and whole foods.

💡 Healthy Eating

A balanced diet can contribute to overall health and wellbeing. Individual dietary requirements can vary.

⚠️ Important

Food does not replace medical treatment. This page provides general educational information and does not claim that any food can cure rheumatoid arthritis.
